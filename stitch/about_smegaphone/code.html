About SMegaphone

Built for academic research and prototype validation.

science Research Intent

SMegaphone is developed as a controlled environment to study secure multi-modal communication patterns in restricted network topologies. It serves as both a practical utility and a data-gathering instrument for ongoing studies in distributed systems.

The architecture leverages a hybrid peer-to-peer and centralized relay model to ensure message integrity and delivery even under simulated high-latency or adversarial network conditions.
